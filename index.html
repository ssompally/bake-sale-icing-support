How to play

Each cake sits on a stand with a recipe card listing its icing layers from bottom to top. Your piping bag holds one dab of icing at a time, and you can see the next few dabs coming. Tap a cake to pipe the current dab onto it — it only sticks if it is the colour that cake needs next. A wrong dab is wasted, so plan ahead using the upcoming dabs.

You win a level when every cake matches its card, and the level ends if you run out of dabs first. There are no timers and no lives — take as long as you like, and every level can be finished without spending anything.

Controls

  • Undo — reverses your last piped dab. The first undo in an attempt is free.
  • Hint — highlights the cake to pipe next. You start with 3 free hints; after that, a hint costs 200 coins.
  • Restart — always free, resets the level from the beginning.
  • Rewind — puts dabs back into your queue when you run out, if you choose to buy it.

Coins and purchases

Coins are earned by winning levels and from daily treats, and can also be bought in packs from the in-game shop, along with a one-time starter bundle. Coins buy optional helpers — an extra dab, a rewind, an extra undo, a hint, or a rainbow-dab booster before a hard level. Bake Sale Icing has no ads, no subscriptions, and no lives to refill. Purchased coins never expire.

If you bought the starter bundle and it is missing, tap Restore Purchases in Settings or at the bottom of the Shop in the app while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection may be required. Coin packs and the starter bundle deliver their contents immediately on purchase. Coin packs are used up when you buy them, so they cannot be restored; Restore Purchases re-delivers the one-time starter bundle if it did not previously complete, but it does not re-grant coins that were already spent.

For an Apple purchase or refund request, visit Apple's purchase support. If a purchase went through but you did not receive its contents, contact us above with the date of purchase.

Saved progress and offline play

Your cake levels, stars, and coin balance are stored on your device. Bake Sale Icing does not have a game account or its own cloud progress sync. Deleting the app removes this local progress, and it cannot be restored; Restore Purchases only re-delivers eligible past purchases, not gameplay progress. Your device's backup settings may affect what comes back after a reinstall.

Levels can be played offline. Buying coins, restoring a purchase, or downloading a new level pack requires an internet connection.

Common questions

A level seems impossible.
Every level is checked solvable before release. If one has you stuck, tell us the level number — that feedback genuinely shapes future updates.
Will I lose progress if I reinstall?
Deleting the app removes its local data, including levels completed and coin balance. Restore Purchases applies to eligible one-time purchases, not to progress or previously spent coins.
