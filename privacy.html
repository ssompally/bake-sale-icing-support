Bake Sale Icing privacy

Last updated September 27, 2026

SupportPrivacy

Raindrop LLC (Raindrop Limited Liability Company) ("we", "us") publishes Bake Sale Icing. Contact help@raindrop.games with privacy questions or requests.

Information on your device

The game stores your level progress, stars, coin balance, settings, and purchase-delivery records on your device. It also keeps a gameplay log on your device (which levels you play and how they go), tagged with a random install ID that the app creates when it is first opened. You can export this log yourself from Settings → Export gameplay data. There is no game account and no app-operated cloud progress sync.

A small backup of your coin balance, items, and purchase-delivery records is kept in this device's Keychain, which stays on this device and lets them come back if you reinstall. Your operating system's backup settings may include this app data.

Gameplay stats and crash reports (only if you choose "Share Stats")

During the first level, after How to play, the game asks once whether you want to share gameplay stats. Nothing is sent to Google unless you tap Share Stats. If you tap "No thanks", or never answer, the app does not contact Firebase at all.

If you tap Share Stats, the app uses these Google services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: gameplay events (levels started and finished, with taps, stars, time, hints and undos used; offers shown and accepted, with your coin balance; session start and length; finishing How to play), in-app purchase events, an app-instance ID, the random install ID, device model, iOS version, app version, language and country. Google derives a general (city-level) location from your IP address; it does not store the IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports (stack traces, device model, iOS version, free memory and disk space), a Firebase installation ID and the random install ID.
  • Firebase Remote Config: fetches game settings such as prices in coins and rewards, using a Firebase installation ID, app version, language, country and time zone.

We use this data to find crashes and to balance levels and prices. It uses a random ID, never your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and all advertising signals are turned off.

Retention: Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Changing your mind: turn off Settings → Share gameplay stats at any time. The app then stops sending Analytics and Crashlytics data, resets its Analytics ID, deletes unsent crash reports, stops attaching the random install ID, and stops fetching Remote Config; from the next launch it does not start Firebase at all. Data sent before you turned it off is kept until the retention period ends. To have it deleted sooner, email us your export from Settings → Export gameplay data; it contains the random install ID we need to find your data.

Read Google's privacy policy and Firebase's privacy information.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card number. The app uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, deliver coin packs and the starter bundle, support restoration, and prevent purchase fraud. RevenueCat also gives us aggregate sales reports.

RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and transaction details under the app's random install ID, not your name or email address. The app contacts RevenueCat when it starts, to check purchase status and available products, even if you have not made a purchase and whether or not you share stats. These requests include your device's identifier for vendors (a device identifier Apple assigns to each developer's apps), which RevenueCat uses for purchase processing and fraud prevention. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Read RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy for details about their services.

Service providers

Google (Firebase), RevenueCat and GitHub process data for us only to provide the services described on this page. They give your data the same or equal protection as this policy describes.

Advertising and tracking

Bake Sale Icing has no advertising SDK and no ads. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not show Apple's tracking prompt because it does not track. We do not sell or share your information for targeted advertising.

Children

Bake Sale Icing is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account creation, chat, or other feature that collects personal information from any player.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information only to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include passwords or payment-card details. We retain support correspondence as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention and your choices

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the app or otherwise clear its data; device backups may retain earlier copies. Deleting the app removes this local data, except the small Keychain backup described above, which iOS keeps on the device. Deleting the app does not cancel or refund a purchase, or erase purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat, or stats already shared with Google.

We retain purchase records as needed to provide purchased content, handle support and disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we may need information to locate your records and verify your request, and some transaction information may need to be retained for legal or service reasons.

This support website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party embedded media. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The app also downloads level and price updates from this site. GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors, and of the app when it checks for updates, for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the updated date above.
