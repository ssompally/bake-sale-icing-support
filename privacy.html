Bake Sale Icing privacy

Last updated September 25, 2026

SupportPrivacy

Raindrop LLC (Raindrop Limited Liability Company) publishes Bake Sale Icing. Contact help@raindrop.games with privacy questions or requests.

Information on your device

The game stores your level progress, stars, coin balance, settings, and purchase-delivery records on your device. Gameplay analytics — such as which levels you play and how you do on them — are recorded only on your device, to help balance the game, and are never automatically uploaded to us. You can choose to export this data yourself from Settings. There is no game account and no app-operated cloud progress sync. Your operating system's backup settings may include this app data.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card number. The app uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, deliver coin packs and the starter bundle, support restoration, and prevent purchase fraud. RevenueCat also gives us aggregate sales reports.

RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and transaction details using an automatically assigned anonymous app user identifier, not your name or email address. The app contacts RevenueCat when it starts, to check purchase status and available products, even if you have not made a purchase. These requests include your device's identifier for vendors (a device identifier Apple assigns to each developer's apps), which RevenueCat uses for purchase processing and fraud prevention. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. An anonymous identifier is not a promise that no data is collected; see RevenueCat's own privacy policy for what it processes on our behalf.

Read RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy for details about their services.

Advertising and tracking

Bake Sale Icing has no advertising SDK, no rewarded ads, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell or share your information for targeted advertising.

Children

Bake Sale Icing is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account creation, chat, or other feature that collects personal information from any player.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information only to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include passwords or payment-card details. We retain support correspondence as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention and your choices

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the app or otherwise clear its data; device backups may retain earlier copies. Deleting the app removes this local data but does not cancel or refund a purchase, or erase purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat.

We retain purchase records as needed to provide purchased content, handle support and disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we may need information to locate your records and verify your request, and some transaction information may need to be retained for legal or service reasons.

This support website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party embedded media. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The app also downloads level and price updates from this site. GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors, and of the app when it checks for updates, for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the updated date above.
